How to verify.

  
Leaf = keccak256(abi.encode(address token, uint8 score, bytes32 keccak256(verdict), bytes32 keccak256(checks), uint64 checkedAtMs)). Pairs are hashed sorted, an odd node moves up unchanged. Take a proof from /api/v1/proof/0x…, fold it into the leaf, and compare with the root in the anchor transaction's data (after the 4 bytes 0x54454c4c).
