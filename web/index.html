07 // FAQ

Before you check.

Is this financial advice?

No. Tell describes what it finds on chain and on the web. It never tells you to buy or sell, and it can be wrong, so every report shows why it scored the way it did.

How is the score calculated?

Each finding adds risk points. Critical findings, like an owner who can mint new tokens or unlocked liquidity, push the score to at least 80 on their own.

Which tokens can I check?

Any ERC-20 on Robinhood Chain. Paste the address, or a DexScreener link to its pool.

Can my trading bot use Tell?

Yes. Connect it to the MCP server at tell.markets/mcp or call /api/v1/check. Quick checks are free; full checks are a few free a day, then paid per check in USDG over x402. See the docs.

Why is a token "low risk" and still dead?

Tell looks for signs of a setup: dangerous contract powers, insiders, a deployer who dumps, a site that lies. Most launches die without anyone cheating. Low risk means no tell was found, not that the token will go up. See how the scores hold up.
