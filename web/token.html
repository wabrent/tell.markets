From launch to burn.

01 // LaunchOn Pons

The whole supply goes into the Pons bonding curve at launch. The token has no mint function, so the supply can only shrink.

02 // GraduateUniswap v4

When the curve fills, liquidity moves to a Uniswap v4 pool. From then on $TELL trades like any other token.

03 // EarnAgents pay

Every full check past the free allowance is paid in USDG over x402 and lands in the treasury, on-chain.

04 // BurnBuy back

The buyback share buys $TELL and sends it to the dead address. Each burn is listed with its transaction.
