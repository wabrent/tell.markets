Does the score mean anything?

How it is measured.

Score
The first score Tell gave the token, before anyone knew how it would end.
Outcome
Each token is looked at once, 2 to 7 days after its first check, and the result is frozen.
Still trading
Has a DEX pool with at least $5,000 of liquidity at that moment.
Source
DexScreener for pools and liquidity; Tell's own score history for scores.
Excluded
Stablecoins, WETH and tokenized stocks Tell recognizes.
Good to know
  • Survival is not safety: a token can be alive and still be a bad buy, or die without any wrongdoing.
  • Most launches never reach a pool, so even low-risk tokens die often. Compare the levels with each other, not with zero.
  • Older scores were made by older versions of Tell; the checks keep improving.
